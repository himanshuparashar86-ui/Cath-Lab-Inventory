Important: manual synchronisation

  1. Export a JSON backup from the device where you last updated stock.
  2. Transfer the file to your other device (USB, email, or a secure file-sharing method).
  3. Open this app on that device and import the backup using Merge.
  4. Export a fresh backup after making changes. Avoid editing both devices independently before merging.

Do not enter patient names, MRNs, dates of birth or other patient-identifiable information. This app is for inventory only.
